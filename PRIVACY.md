# Privacy Policy — One More Tap

**Effective date: 2 October 2026**

One More Tap ("the app") is a reflex game. This policy explains what data is collected.

## Data we collect

**Purchase history.** When you buy One More Tap Pro, the purchase is processed by the App Store and recorded by RevenueCat (our billing provider) under an anonymous app user ID. No name, email, or payment details are seen or stored by us. Purchase records are used solely to unlock Pro features.

**That's it.** The app:

- Does not require an account
- Does not collect or transmit your location, contacts, photos, or usage analytics
- Stores your scores, streaks, and theme choice only on your device (never uploaded)
- Does not use advertising or tracking SDKs
- Does not track you across other apps or websites

## Third-party services

- **Apple App Store / StoreKit** — processes payments. [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- **RevenueCat** — manages purchase entitlements. [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.
