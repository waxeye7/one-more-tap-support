# Privacy Policy for One More Tap

**Effective date: 15 October 2026**

One More Tap ("the app") is a reflex game for iPhone and Android. It is made by Merlin Robin Stemmer ("I", "me", the developer). This policy explains what data the app handles, on both platforms.

## Short version

- No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and I do not sell data.
- Your scores, streaks and settings stay on your device.
- Two things leave your device: a routine app update check (both platforms) and, on iPhone only, purchase records for the Pro upgrade.

## What stays on your device

The app saves your best score, today's best, streak, games played, best combo and your theme choice on your device. This data is never sent to me or to anyone else.

## What leaves your device

**App update check (iPhone and Android).** When the app opens, it can check Expo's update service (expo.dev) for a newer version of the app's code. The request includes a random install identifier that the app generates, the app version, the device platform (iOS or Android), and your IP address, which any internet request carries. This identifier is not your name, your email or your advertising ID, and it is not used for ads. Expo processes these requests on my behalf. [Expo's privacy policy](https://expo.dev/privacy)

**Purchases (iPhone only).** Pro is a one-time purchase available only on iPhone through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. The purchase is recorded by RevenueCat, my purchase provider, under an anonymous app user ID so the app can unlock Pro. I do not see or store your name, email or payment details. Purchase records are used only to unlock Pro. [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

**Android has no purchases.** The Android app does not offer Pro or any purchase, and it does not contact a purchase provider.

## Share card

When you tap Share Score, the app makes a score image on your device and hands it to the app you choose, such as Messages or email. I never receive that image. What the app you choose does with it is covered by that app's own policy.

## What the app does not do

The app:

- Has no account or sign-in
- Shows no ads and does not use advertising identifiers
- Does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data
- Does not include analytics or crash reporting tools that send data about you
- Does not track you across other apps or websites
- Does not sell or share your data for advertising

Your phone's own backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may copy the app's saved data. That is handled by Apple or Google under their terms, not by me.

## Retention and deletion

Scores, streaks and settings live only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. I hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing on my side to delete. Update check requests are kept by Expo for a limited time under its own policy. If you have a question or request about your data, email the contact below.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
